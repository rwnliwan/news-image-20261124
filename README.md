One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn as a newspaper front page with six stories. The masthead names the day, and each panel pairs a picture with a short headline and one line of fact, so a reader who missed the day can tell what happened from the image alone.

The six stories, each checked against a reported source on 24 November before it was drawn:

1. **CAS to rule today on the AFCON title.** The Court of Arbitration for Sport decides on Tuesday afternoon in Lausanne on Senegal's appeal against the title going to Morocco, after Senegal won the January final 1-0 on the pitch. The verdict was still pending when this image was drawn; a newspaper report saying Morocco would keep the title was unconfirmed by CAS, so the page keeps the story as pending.
2. **Chess's youngest title match begins.** Defending champion Gukesh Dommaraju, 20, meets challenger Javokhir Sindarov, 20, in Game 1 in Geneva, the youngest World Chess Championship match in history, for a $2.5m prize fund. Sindarov has White in Game 1.
3. **Huang back at Nvidia.** Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday; shares jumped 31% and the Nasdaq rose about 6%.
4. **24 dead, about 30 missing.** The search goes on at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Cikande, Banten, Indonesia, after Saturday's fire; 24 deaths were confirmed on Monday and about 30 people are still missing.
5. **Moscow warns Estonia.** Russia's Foreign Ministry said Estonia's planned closure of its land border from 1 December puts the two countries "on the verge of triggering open war".
6. **Gaza a toxic wasteland.** An Oxfam and Conflict and Environment Observatory report finds indicators of potential contamination across more than 99% of the Gaza Strip, with air, soil and water poisoned for decades to come.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png` to the output directory. The image is a 1024x1024 JPEG (369,787 bytes, inside the 1 MiB limit), and both runs write byte-identical output.
